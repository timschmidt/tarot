Tarot engineering handoff

Status and scope

Verified facts below come from the linked upstream documentation, checked on 28 October 2026. Everything described as a Tarot behavior, integration choice, or acceptance criterion is proposed. No Tarot repository, running compositor, or target image was inspected. These notes define a contract for implementation rather than documenting existing code.

Verified platform

PINE64 specifies a 720 by 1440 portrait display, nominally 6 inches with an 18:9 aspect ratio, and 4 GB RAM. Its documented external controls are power and volume; there is no Palm gesture-area device. Source: https://pine64.org/documentation/PinePhone_Pro/Further_information/Specifications/ and https://pine64.org/devices/pinephone_pro/

Nura is the project formerly called postmarketOS. UI packages may support OpenRC, systemd, or both. Tarot must declare and test its supported configurations; no service-manager choice is assumed here. Sources: https://nura.eco/blog/2026/09/27/nura-rename/ and https://nura.eco/edge/2026/07/10/explicit-service-managers/

Proposed integration tiers

Tier 1 comprises shell-owned surfaces: cards, launcher, menus, dashboard, lock presentation, and system prompts. These can implement the complete Tarot interaction contract.

Tier 2 comprises cooperating applications or explicit adapters. They can expose application back navigation, menu commands, and meaningful window relationships. Document support per application; do not infer navigation from a window title.

Tier 3 comprises ordinary unadapted Wayland applications. Tarot supplies composition, focus, card navigation, and window lifecycle, while the application retains its internal controls. Wayland clients draw their own buffers; compositor styling cannot automatically transform arbitrary GTK or Qt content. Core xdg-shell does not supply universal application-back or exported-menu semantics. Sources: https://wayland.freedesktop.org/architecture.html and https://smithay.github.io/smithay/wayland_protocols/xdg/shell/server/xdg_toplevel/struct.XdgToplevel.html

Proposed service map

Launcher: use freedesktop desktop entries, including declared D-Bus activation, for names, icons, and launch actions. https://specifications.freedesktop.org/desktop-entry/latest/

Connectivity: use NetworkManager for connection state, saved networks, activation, and secrets-agent integration. https://networkmanager.dev/docs/developers/

Telephony: use ModemManager for modem, SIM, registration, voice, call, and SMS state. Delegate hardware-specific modem power management to the distribution. https://www.freedesktop.org/software/ModemManager/doc/latest/ModemManager/ref-dbus.html

Audio: use PipeWire and WirePlumber policy for volumes, routes, and call-profile integration. Do not introduce callaudiod as a new dependency: its upstream README marks it deprecated. https://pipewire.pages.freedesktop.org/wireplumber/ and https://gitlab.com/mobian1/callaudiod/-/blob/master/README.md

Bluetooth: use BlueZ Adapter1, Device1, and pairing-agent interfaces. https://github.com/bluez/bluez/blob/master/doc/org.bluez.Adapter.rst and https://github.com/bluez/bluez/blob/master/doc/org.bluez.AgentManager.rst

Power: use UPower for battery reporting and the selected distribution's session/power backend, including login1 where provided, for suspend and inhibitors. https://upower.freedesktop.org/docs/Device.html and https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/262/org.freedesktop.login1.html

Dashboard: own the session's org.freedesktop.Notifications service; preserve replacement IDs, actions, and expiry semantics. Use MPRIS for compatible media-player controls. https://specifications.freedesktop.org/notification/latest/basic-design.html and https://specifications.freedesktop.org/mpris/latest/

Prompts: integrate compatible XDG portal backends and a session polkit agent. Service authorization remains authoritative. https://flatpak.github.io/xdg-desktop-portal/docs/ and https://www.freedesktop.org/software/polkit/docs/latest/polkit.8

Proposed input and lifecycle rules

Use 360 by 720 logical units at integer scale 2 as the initial portrait baseline. This is a Tarot choice, not a measured webOS dimension. Reserve an explicit shell-owned gesture region. Touches beginning there must not also activate application content. Establish ownership before dispatch; preserve cancellation and never deliver an incomplete gesture as a surprise application tap. Define landscape and keyboard-visible behavior separately.

Pin keyboard integration to the protocols supported by the selected on-screen keyboard and client toolkits. A common integration uses text-input-v3 with input-method-v2; verify actual versions. Restrict input-method access to trusted clients, update usable content bounds, maintain composition state, and return focus correctly after menus or prompts. Smithay documents an input-method client filter: https://smithay.github.io/smithay/src/smithay/wayland/input_method/mod.rs.html

An upward card dismissal requests xdg_toplevel.close; it does not authorize process termination. The client may refuse or open a save dialog. Keep sufficient card state to reconcile that response, restore the relevant surface when necessary, and expose force-quit only as an explicit exceptional action. The xdg-shell source linked above documents this close contract.

Locking must hide normal content and stop normal-client input. A lock-client crash must not unlock the session. Establish the locked frame before suspend, mask private previews, and treat any drag-to-unlock gesture as entry to configured authentication. https://isaacfreund.com/docs/wayland/ext-session-lock-v1/

Proposed asynchronous state contract

Render from observed service state. A request creates a pending state; success appears only after backend confirmation. Distinguish connecting, connected, disconnected, disabled, unavailable, and permission denied where applicable. Call connection and audio-route readiness are separate facts. Identify a hardware privacy switch only when the backend establishes that cause. Give failed or timed-out actions a recoverable explanation. Subscribe to changes, reconnect after service restarts, and never block the compositor on D-Bus or network work.

Proposed launch blockers

Block release for credential bypass, private content exposed during resume, duplicated shell/application gesture handling, lost unsaved work during card dismissal, or service waits that stall input or animation. Also require working keyboard composition and focus recovery, reliable application launch/failure handling, readable geometry on the actual panel, and validated incoming-call behavior while locked and after suspend. Test the installed firmware, kernel, and service configuration rather than assuming another distribution's results apply. Current platform maturity statements are device-dependent: https://nura.eco/state/ and https://pine64.org/documentation/PinePhone_Pro/Software/Software_state/
